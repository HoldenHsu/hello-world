## Talk outline (~75 s/slide)

1. **Title** — three pillars motif: Vaccinate · Cure · Screen
2. **The problem** — EU-27: ~54,600 deaths (2022), 6th cancer killer, ~12% 5-yr survival; Europe-region deaths 43,000 → 69,000 since 2000
3. **The paradox** — ≥60% preventable (Lancet Commission 2025), causes known, proven test — yet liver excluded from the 2022 EU Council screening recommendation
4. **Taiwan's baseline** — 15–20% HBsAg carriers; Beasley 1981: ~100× HCC risk in carriers
5. **Pillar 1: Vaccinate** — 1984 world-first universal newborn HBV vaccination; carrier rate 9.78% → 0.64%; −69% HCC (JNCI 2009)
6. **Pillar 2: Cure** — DAAs free since 2017; 182,000+ treated, 98.3% SVR; WHO 2030 HCV goal reached in 2025 (90.2% diagnosed / 90.6% treated)
7. **Pillar 3: Screen** — free lifetime HBV/HCV test ages 39–79 (7.55 M screened); q6mo US+AFP; −31% HCC mortality (Changhua); BCLC-0 detection 6.2% → 11.3%
8. **The payoff** — 14 consecutive years of falling incidence; 57.8 → 45.0/100k (2013–21); chronic liver disease out of top-10 causes of death
9. **Lessons** — single-payer financing, registry call–recall, home-grown cohorts, sequenced pillars
10. **Europe is different** — HCV legacy + alcohol (50–60% of HCC in CEE) + MASLD (#1 in Sweden; 35–50% non-cirrhotic)
11. **The EU gap** — 5.4 M chronic HBV/HCV, up to 85–89% undiagnosed; surveillance uptake ~39% real-world; 60% of UK cases incurable at presentation
12. **Strategy 1: Find & treat** — lifetime test, reflex+registry, micro-elimination, free treatment; target 90/90 by 2030
13. **Strategy 2: Smart surveillance** — aMAP/PAGE-B triage, GALAD, abbreviated MRI (86% sens.), AI ultrasound, LIVERAIM (100k, 6 countries)
14. **Strategy 3: Non-viral frontier** — alcohol policy (7.1% of EU cancers), MASLD case-finding (LiverScreen); 1-in-3 liver cancers from alcohol+MASH by 2050
15. **Roadmap 2026–2040** — Prove & prepare → Organise → Deliver; KPIs: 90/90, >50% early-stage, mortality bent by 2035
16. **Conclusions** — "Liver cancer is a policy-sensitive disease. Taiwan wrote the proof of concept; the EU can write the sequel."
17. **Key references** — for the proceedings
